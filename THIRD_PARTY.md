- **Kartverket DTM1 — Hornøya**, ©Kartverket, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): `src/data/hornoya.json` derives from a 1200 m crop of [tile 33-179-197](https://nedlasting.geonorge.no/hoydedata/DTM1/33-179-197.tif). Land connected to the crop boundary is removed; water is synthetic depth; retained elevations and horizontal distances have no exaggeration. The derivative stores 769² vertices at 1.5625 m spacing. Source coordinates, extracted-crop hash, reviewed header hash, source ETag and unconfirmed vertical datum are retained in the data. `scripts/fetch-hornoya.ts` reproduces the crop using bounded HTTP ranges; `npm run import:northeast` prepares the derivative. [Provider terms](https://www.kartverket.no/api-og-data/vilkar-for-bruk/). Vegetation, rocks and water are game scenery, not a survey of Hornøya.

- **Kartverket DTM1 — Trenyken**, ©Kartverket, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): `src/data/trenyken.json` derives from a 1200 m crop of [tile 33-131-170](https://nedlasting.geonorge.no/hoydedata/DTM1/33-131-170.tif). The derivative retains 769² vertices at 1.5625 m spacing without horizontal or vertical exaggeration. Land connected to the crop boundary is removed and water is synthetic depth. Full-tile and extracted-crop SHA-256 hashes, source coordinates and the unconfirmed vertical datum are retained in the data; `npm run import:northwest` reproduces it from the pinned tile. [Provider terms](https://www.kartverket.no/api-og-data/vilkar-for-bruk/). Game scenery and map placement do not reconstruct the surveyed island's environment.
